Why we started آواز

Most children see their speech therapist once a week, if they are lucky. The real progress happens on the other six days, at home.

That is where families often feel unsure. Parents want to help but don't know what to practise, and therapists can't see what happened between visits.

We care for parents too. Watching your child struggle to speak can be exhausting and lonely, so Awaaz offers psychological support to help parents cope, alongside emotional care for every child.

Why so many children never get help

Most families don't skip therapy because they don't care. They skip it because it is hard to afford, hard to fit in, hard to reach, and hard on the child.

💸

It costs too much

Weekly sessions add up fast, and extra visits are out of reach for many families.

With Awaaz: daily practice happens at home, so every paid session goes further and fewer extra visits are needed.

⏰

There is no time

Travel, traffic, waiting rooms and time off work turn one session into half a day.

With Awaaz: available 24/7. Do therapy at your own ease, whenever it suits you. No travel, no waiting room.

📍

It is too far away

Families in small towns and remote areas often have no speech therapist nearby at all.

With Awaaz: all you need is a good internet connection and a device. Practise on your own, and get assessed by a professional therapist online.

💭

It feels heavy

Clinics can feel scary, and children who struggle to speak often feel shy, judged or "different".

With Awaaz: practice happens at home, with a parent, through play. Safe, private and calm.

Feelings matter as much as sounds

A speech difficulty is never just about speech. Children who struggle to be understood can lose confidence, avoid talking in class, get teased, or carry frustration they can't explain.

So Awaaz is built to protect how a child feels, not just to correct how they sound.

  • Every try is praised. No buzzers or harsh "wrong" sounds, just a soft hint to try again.
  • Small wins are celebrated. Stars, badges and streaks show progress a child can see and feel proud of.
  • Parents learn to encourage. Lessons remind them to stay patient, praise effort and stop when the child is tired.
  • The child sets the pace. Short sessions and breaks, so practice never turns into pressure.
  • Therapists see the whole child. Notes on sensitivities, learning style and what helps the child sit next to the scores.

About us

Awaaz means "voice" in Urdu. We are a small team in Pakistan building speech therapy software for children with speech and articulation difficulties.

Many families can only see a therapist once a week, and most apps are English only, loud and busy. We wanted something calm, bilingual and simple enough for any parent to use at home, so practice continues between sessions.

🎯

Our mission

Make daily speech practice possible for every child, at home, in their own language.

🤝

The best therapists, on board

We bring experienced speech therapists on board, so every child is assessed by a professional. Our advisory board guides the content, and more therapists are joining.
